## Vercel find-skills

The task-oriented skill discovery and search/install examples were informed by **find-skills**, part of **vercel-labs/skills**. ReuseBeacon adds installed-skill reuse, bounded searches, source inspection, scoped installation, collision protection, and evidence of actual use. It does not adopt leaderboard-first discovery, numerical popularity thresholds, or global installation defaults. It does not bundle or require find-skills and claims no endorsement.
